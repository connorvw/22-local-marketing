Every marketing dollar should leave you with something stronger.

Paid ads can create demand now. SEO, reviews, a useful website, customer data, and good follow-up make that demand more valuable over time. The point is not to avoid paid traffic. The point is to stop depending on a marketplace that owns the customer and sells the same opportunity again.
