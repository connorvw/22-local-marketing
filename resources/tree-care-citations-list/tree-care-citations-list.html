The Tree Care Citations List

Where your business needs to be listed, in priority order  |  22 Local Marketing

A citation is any place online that lists your business name, address, and phone number. Google and AI search tools cross-check these listings to decide whether you are real, established, and local. A consistent citation footprint quietly lifts map rankings; an inconsistent one quietly caps them. This is the list we work from, in the order we build it.

The rule that matters more than the list: identical everywhere.

Same business name, same address format, same phone number, on every single listing. "Moberly's Tree Service" on one site and "Moberlys Tree Svc LLC" on another reads as two different businesses to a machine. Fix inconsistencies before building anything new.

Tier 3: Local and Everywhere Else

  • Local news and community sites. Sponsorships, event coverage, and "best of" lists in your actual town outweigh any national directory.
  • Suppliers and partners. Equipment dealers, nurseries, and landscapers you work with often have partner pages. Real relationships make the best citations.
  • City and county business registries. Free, official, and trusted by machines.

How to Work the List

  • Fix before you build. Run a free listing scan, correct every mismatched name, address, and phone first.
  • One listing per site, ever. Duplicates hurt more than missing listings. Claim and merge duplicates before adding anything.
  • Write a unique description for each. Pasting the same paragraph 40 times looks automated. Vary it honestly.
  • Track it. Keep a simple sheet: site, login, date, status. Future you will be grateful.
  • Never buy bulk citation blasts. 500 spam directories for $50 creates cleanup work, not rankings.
What this looks like in practice

Tier 1 in the first month, the free half of Tier 2 in the second, then a slow, tracked drip of the rest. Done once, kept consistent, it compounds quietly for years. This is included in every plan we run, but nothing on this list requires us.
