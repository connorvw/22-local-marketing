Digital Presence Audit

Moberly's Tree Service

July 2026  |  Pueblo West, Colorado  |  Prepared by 22 Local Marketing
Moberly's Tree Service has one of the strongest reputations in the Pueblo market, a 4.9 rating across 47 Google reviews with an active, well-maintained Business Profile. The website is the weak link. It is a 7-page builder site with no service pages, no location pages, and zero measurable Google search visibility, so the business is winning on reputation and losing on reach. The single most important move is replacing the current site with a full service-and-location page structure that lets Google connect this reputation to the searches happening in Pueblo and Colorado Springs.

Gap Analysis

No service or location pages vs 700+ monthly searchesGoogle ranks pages, not businesses. With one homepage covering every service and city, Moberly's cannot appear for "stump grinding Pueblo" or anything Colorado Springs. This is the highest-impact gap.
4.9-star reputation undersold on the websiteReviews appear in a feedback section low on the page, but the aggregate proof (4.9 stars, 47 reviews) never appears above the fold, and with no review schema those stars can't show in search results either.
Zero schema markupNo LocalBusiness or Service structured data, so Google gets no machine-readable confirmation of who they are, where they work, or what they do.
Review count trails the leaders47 vs 131 (Apex), 78 (Sandoval), 75 (Rams). At their current pace the gap widens. A simple post-job review ask closes it within a year.
Colorado Springs claimed but not backedThe GBP posts and site text mention Colorado Springs constantly, but with no location page, no reviews mentioning it, and a Pueblo West address, Google doesn't yet have the signals it needs to surface them there.
