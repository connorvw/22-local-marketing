Ranking in AI Search

Cliff Notes

A growing share of customers skip the search results and ask AI directly: Google's AI Overview, ChatGPT, Gemini. The AI reads everything public about local companies and recommends one or two by name, favoring deep, consistent, regularly updated information. You found us through AI search; your customers will find you the same way.

Your Problem

AI has almost nothing to read about Moberly's: seven pages, no structured data, conflicting basics (your site says closed weekends, your Google profile says open). Ask AI for a tree service in Pueblo West or Colorado Springs and it doesn't yet have the information it needs to recommend you.

The Fix

We give AI what it rewards: full site structure, monthly educational content, consistent business data everywhere, and schema markup that lists your services, cities, and 4.9 rating in machine-readable form.

Proof: Better Way Land Management

Ohio land management client. Google's AI Overview now names them first, cites their website twice, and closes by recommending their free estimates. Their leads went from 1 a month in January to 28 a month by June.
